Our story

Small beginnings. A shared purpose.

AnnaSeva Foundation was born from one simple belief: no one should ever go to bed hungry.

What began as a community initiative has grown into a movement powered by compassion, volunteers, and generous donors. Every meal served is possible because someone chose kindness.

We prepare and distribute fresh, nourishing meals with care, meeting people with the respect and warmth they deserve.
