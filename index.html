Frequently asked questions

Is my donation secure?

Yes. Online donations are processed securely through Razorpay using encrypted payment methods.

Will I receive an 80G receipt?

Eligible donations receive an official 80G receipt after payment verification.

Can I donate using UPI or bank transfer?

Absolutely. You can donate via Razorpay, direct bank transfer, or by scanning the UPI QR code.

How is my donation used?

Every contribution supports food preparation, distribution, and Annadanam activities.
